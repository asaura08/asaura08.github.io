## Context and responsibility

My work involved assessing the potential impact of a web application finding and reporting it responsibly.

## Decisions and follow-up

I documented the observations, explained the potential impact and reported the issue to the organization. After an extended period without a response, I escalated it to INCIBE-CERT.

## Outcome and lessons

The documented outcome is the report and its escalation. Communicating the finding required separating observations from estimated impact and protecting the information of those affected.
