## Getting data into the CRM

The work started before anyone contacted a business. Information had to be collected, organised and made available to the person following up. At MetaZenIT I worked on an integration between Google Maps, n8n and Frappe CRM that connected these steps.

## Preparing follow-up

Another part of the work used the collected information to prepare AI-assisted communications. The result was an integration for internal use. Its changes are documented in the project’s development history and pull request reviews.
