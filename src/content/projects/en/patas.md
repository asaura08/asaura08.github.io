## From catalogue to shelter

A family views a public listing and applies to adopt. The shelter receives the application in its inbox and can assign it or reserve the animal.

## Product decision

Server-sent events keep different dashboard sessions in sync. Reads check the user’s shelter membership. The project has been tested in development.
