## From an enquiry to a service

A client commissioned a platform to connect companies looking for services with professionals who could provide them. I contributed to product development and helped define how businesses and individuals would use their respective portals. The work covered customer records, sales leads and account access.

## Scope of the design

The design called for subscriptions, service provisioning and connections to external providers. One important question was what should happen between a customer starting a subscription and gaining access to the service they had ordered. Parts of the architecture remained proposals. The final delivery and production status are unconfirmed.
